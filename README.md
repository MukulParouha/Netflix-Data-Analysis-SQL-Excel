# 🎥 Netflix Business Performance Data Analysis (SQL + Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview
This is an End-to-End Data Analyst Portfolio Project analyzing a comprehensive dataset of 60,000 Netflix subscribers. The primary objective of this project is to solve real-world business challenges—such as customer churn, revenue leakage, plan-wise tier performance, and user demographics—by leveraging data-driven insights and presenting them via an interactive executive dashboard.

- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): \$572 (Average monthly monetization per user)

- Payment Success Rate: 89% (Transaction success threshold)

## 💡 Core Business Problems & Data-Driven Solutions

### 1. Revenue Engine Analysis (Plan Performance Optimization)
- Business Problem: Identifying which subscription tier drives the highest business value to optimize marketing spend allocation.
- Data Solution: The data revealed that the Premium & Standard Plans collectively generate 77% of the total revenue. 
- Recommendation: Marketing budgets should focus heavily on targeted cross-selling campaigns to upgrade Basic and Mobile tier users into these high-value brackets.

### 2. Plugging Revenue Leaks (Combating Involuntary Churn via Payment Failures)
- Business Problem: Uncovering the root cause behind the 7% customer churn rate.
- Data Solution: Analysis indicated an 11% payment failure/friction rate (only an 89% success rate). This transaction failure acts as a direct catalyst for involuntary customer churn.
- Recommendation: Optimizing the payment gateway infrastructure and implementing automated smart-retry logic can instantly recover lost transactions and potentially drop the churn rate down to 3%.

### 3. User Segmentation & Content Strategy (Demographic Insights)
- Business Problem: Mapping subscriber demographics to provide clear directions for future content procurement.
- Data Solution: The subscriber distribution shows that Female users slightly outnumber male subscribers.
- Recommendation: Future content acquisition and regional original series development should prioritize scripts and genres favored by this dominant demographic segment to enhance engagement.
